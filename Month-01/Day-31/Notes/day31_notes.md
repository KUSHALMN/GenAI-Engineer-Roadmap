Over the past 31 days, we have transitioned from foundational Python/FastAPI/SQL patterns to building battle-tested, enterprise-scale GenAI systems.

  - Day 25: Structured outputs, Pydantic V2 schemas & JSON repair.

  - Day 31: Enterprise Autonomous Support Copilot capstone + LFU Cache DSA in Java.

## 🎯 Capstone Architecture Takeaways
Building production AI is not merely about writing prompts; it is about building **resilient, observable, cost-aware software systems** around non-deterministic probabilistic models.
