## 🤖 Generative AI & Tool Calling

### Research Papers & Documentation
1. **Gorilla: Large Language Model Connected with Massive APIs**
   - *Paper:* [https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15334)
   - *Description:* Landmark research demonstrating how to teach LLMs to accurately select and invoke hundreds of API functions.
2. **Toolformer: Language Models Can Teach Themselves to Use Tools** (Schick et al.)
   - *Paper:* [https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04761)
   - *Description:* Foundational paper exploring self-supervised learning of tool invocation in language models.
3. **ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models** (Yao et al.)
   - *Paper:* [https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629)
   - *Description:* Interleaving thought, action, and observation cycles in LLM agents.
4. **OpenAI Function Calling Guide**
   - *Docs:* [https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/function-calling](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/function-calling)
5. **Groq Tool Use Documentation**
   - *Docs:* [https://console.groq.com/docs/tool-use](https://console.groq.com/docs/tool-use)
