## 📄 Seminal AI Research Papers

1. **ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models**
   - *Authors:* Shunyu Yao, Jeffrey Zhao, Dian Yu, Nan Du, Izhak Shafran, Karthik Narasimhan, Yuan Cao (2022)
   - *Paper:* [arXiv:2210.03629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629)
   - *Key Topic:* Interleaving reasoning traces and task-specific actions for agentic grounding.

2. **Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning**
   - *Authors:* Noah Shinn, Federico Cassano, Edward Berman, Ashwin Gopinath, Karthik Narasimhan, Shunyu Yao (2023)
   - *Paper:* [arXiv:2303.11366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366)
   - *Key Topic:* Dynamic self-reflection and episodic memory for autonomous agents.

3. **LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models**
   - *Authors:* Edward J. Hu, Yelong Shen, Phillip Wallis, Zeyuan Allen-Zhu, Yuanzhi Li, Shean Wang, Lu Wang, Weizhu Chen (2021)
   - *Paper:* [arXiv:2106.09685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685)
   - *Key Topic:* Weight decomposition $W_0 + BA$, rank adaptation, and parameter efficiency.

4. **Training Compute-Optimal Large Language Models (Chinchilla)**
   - *Authors:* Jordan Hoffmann, Sebastian Borgeaud, Arthur Mensch, et al. (DeepMind, 2022)
   - *Paper:* [arXiv:2203.15556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15556)
   - *Key Topic:* Scaling laws, compute budget $C \approx 6ND$, and token-to-parameter ratios.

5. **DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report**
   - *Authors:* DeepSeek-AI (2024)
   - *Repository:* [DeepSeek-V3 GitHub](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V3)
   - *Key Topic:* Mixture-of-Experts (MoE), Multi-head Latent Attention (MLA), and auxiliary-loss-free routing.
