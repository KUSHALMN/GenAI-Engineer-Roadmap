- **Cyclic Sort Invariant:** Only swap when `nums[i] != nums[nums[i] - 1]` to prevent infinite loops on duplicate numbers.
- **Monotonic Deque Window:** Deque maintains decreasing elements; `peekFirst()` always stores the maximum element in the active $K$-window.
