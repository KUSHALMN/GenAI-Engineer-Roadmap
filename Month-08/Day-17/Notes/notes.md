# 📝 Notes — Day 17: AI Governance System Cards & 2D DP

- **Model Cards as Code:** System cards must be version-controlled JSON/YAML schemas checked in CI/CD before artifact promotion to staging.
- **Audit Trails:** Immutable hashes of training datasets and eval results ensure provenance verification during regulatory audits.
- **2D DP Cache Locality:** Single-array 1D compression reduces memory allocations and cache misses during matrix traversals.
