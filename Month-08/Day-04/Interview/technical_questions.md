# 🎯 Technical Interview Questions — Day 04: LLM-as-a-Judge & Sliding Window

### 1. What are the common failure modes of LLM-as-a-Judge, and how do you calibrate it?
**Answer:**
1. **Position Bias:** The judge favors the first response (Model A). *Mitigation:* Evaluate both permutations (A/B and B/A) and take the intersection.
2. **Verbosity Bias:** The judge equates longer, verbose answers with higher quality. *Mitigation:* Penalize answers exceeding length guidelines in the judge prompt rubric.
3. **Self-Preference Bias:** A model (e.g., GPT-4) rates outputs generated by itself higher than competitors. *Mitigation:* Use cross-model ensemble judges (e.g., Claude 3.5 + GPT-4o).

### 2. Explain the window contraction invariant in LC 3 Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters.
**Answer:**
When character `s[right]` was seen previously at index `lastSeen[ch]`, the window left boundary must advance to `Math.max(left, lastSeen[ch] + 1)`. The `Math.max` is critical so that `left` never jumps backward to duplicate characters outside the current active window.
