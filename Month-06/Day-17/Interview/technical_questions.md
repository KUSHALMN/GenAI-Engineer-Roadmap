# Technical Interview Preparation — Multilingual Voice & Dijkstra
1. Code-Switching in ASR: Occurs when speakers alternate languages within a single utterance. Requires multilingual vocabularies and language-agnostic acoustic models (e.g. Whisper, mSLAM).
2. Dijkstra Non-Negative Weight Invariant: Greedy extraction from Min-Heap is optimal only when edge weights $\ge 0$.
