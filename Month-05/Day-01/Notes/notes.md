# Day 01 Notes: Fine-Tuning Decision Framework

## 1. Adaptation Spectrum
- **Prompting / Few-Shot:** Immediate, zero training cost, bounded by context window.
- **RAG:** External dynamic knowledge, verifiable citations, zero parameter updates.
- **SFT (Supervised Fine-Tuning):** Teaches format, domain terminology, specialized syntax, and style.
- **RLHF / DPO:** Aligns models with human preferences and safety policies.
